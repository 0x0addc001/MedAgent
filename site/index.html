SYSTEM / 06

One loop. Six layers.

From raw signals to measurable behavior, each layer contributes a distinct part of the Agent system.

01 / DATA

Clinical context

EHR, guidelines, intake dialogues and red-flag rules.

02 / MODELS

Evidence & risk

Medical LLM, clinical retrieval and risk classification.

03 / TRAINING

Clinical safety

Clinical dialogue SFT and safety preference learning.

04 / INFERENCE

Guided intake

Streaming intake, evidence retrieval and triage.

05 / HARNESS

Clinical workflow

EHR, scheduling, escalation and clinician review.

06 / EVAL

Safety evidence

Clinical safety, triage accuracy, hallucination and bias.

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Built to stay trustworthy.

The Agent is only as useful as the boundaries around it are clear.

01

Safety overrides helpfulness

Red-flag cases escalate through deterministic rules.

02

Cite clinical evidence

Evidence-based statements remain traceable to sources.

03

Clinician in the loop

Qualified review is required for consequential decisions.
